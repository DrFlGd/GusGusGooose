Gus Gus Goose 0.4.0: the bear joins the goose and the otter. Pick it in the top bar, and every accessory tool works on the bear too.

**The bear.** Brown, with black eyes and nose. The bottom is trimmed 2.5 mm so it stands flat on a desk.

- **Head magnet:** the goose's 10 x 4 mm disc, in the middle of the top of the head under at least 1.2 mm of plastic. It slides in after printing through a channel straight up from the flat base, and a brown key fills the channel, flush with the base. The key is about 109 mm long; if you print it standing, give it a brim.
- **Paw magnets:** an open pocket under each paw for an 8 x 4 mm disc. Push the magnet in from below and glue it. Whatever you mount there looks held under the paw. The pockets face down, so the slicer's support under the paws will reach into them.
- **Bear tab:** builds the slide-in bear with the same three versions and tolerance controls as the goose and the otter. Its Print 3MF is painted (brown is filament 1, black 2), with the head key beside the bear.
- **Accessories:** fit, paint and export accessories on the bear's head and paws; a paw accessory gets a pocket for its 8 mm magnet by default. Clothing and the neck band stay goose-only.

**Portable, no installer.** Download the file for your system, unzip it where you want the app to live, and run it. Saved builds go in `Gus Gus Goose Library` next to the app; Settings (the gear) can move the library.

- **Windows:** `…-windows-portable.zip`. SmartScreen will warn that the app is unrecognised because it isn't signed. Click *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **Linux:** `…-linux-x86_64.AppImage`. Run `chmod +x` on it, then start it.
