Gus Gus Goose 0.4.1: a fix for the slide-in goose's head channel.

**Thicker face beside the beak.** On a printed goose the head magnet's channel showed through the white face just above the beak, on both sides. The channel's square upper corners left only 0.2 to 0.4 mm of plastic there. The channel and its white key now follow the magnet's own rounded outline, with a flat top so the key still prints lying flat, and the opening sits 0.26 mm toward the goose's left to even out the sides. The wall behind the opening is now about 1.1 mm at 2 mm in and 1.5 mm at 4 mm in, more than double what it was. The tongue on the key and the beak's pocket for it are unchanged. Rebuild the slide-in goose from the Goose tab and reprint the body and the head key.

**Portable, no installer.** Download the file for your system, unzip it where you want the app to live, and run it. Saved builds go in `Gus Gus Goose Library` next to the app; Settings (the gear) can move the library.

- **Windows:** `…-windows-portable.zip`. SmartScreen will warn that the app is unrecognised because it isn't signed. Click *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **Linux:** `…-linux-x86_64.AppImage`. Run `chmod +x` on it, then start it.
